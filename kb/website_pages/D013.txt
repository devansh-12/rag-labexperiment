Government Scholarships
Eligible students can avail MahaDBT scholarships including Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojna (EBC), Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Vasatigruh Nirvah Bhatta Yojna for hostel maintenance, and post-matric scholarships for SC, ST, OBC, and Minority communities.

Institutional and Merit Grants
SPIT Alumni Association and Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan provide merit-cum-means financial assistance for needy students with exemplary academic records, ensuring that no meritorious candidate drops out due to financial constraints.
