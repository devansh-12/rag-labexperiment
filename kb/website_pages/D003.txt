Board of Governors
The Governing Body is headed by prominent industrialists, educators, and representatives from Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, AICTE, Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), and Mumbai University. It provides strategic direction, oversees fiscal health, and approves key academic and infrastructural expansions.

Academic Council
The Academic Council is the apex academic decision-making body of the autonomous institute. It approves curricula formulated by the Boards of Studies (BoS) for each department, ratifies examination results, introduces new specializations, and ensures compliance with UGC and AICTE guidelines.
