Founding and Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan
Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan was founded in 1938 by Dr. K. M. Munshi with the blessings of Mahatma Gandhi. Sardar Patel College of Engineering (SPCE) was established in 1962 as a government-aided institute. In 1995, Bhavan's trust established SPIT as an unaided engineering wing offering Electronics Engineering, Computer Engineering, and Information Technology.

Milestones and Growth
In 2005, SPIT became an independent entity separated from SPCE. Postgraduate Master of Computer Applications (MCA) was introduced in 2008, followed by M.Tech degrees in Computer Engineering and Electronics & Telecommunication. In 2021, SPIT introduced new undergraduate courses in Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science) and Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
