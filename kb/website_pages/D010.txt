First Year Foundations
The department lays the mathematical and scientific foundation for all first-year engineering students. Courses include Engineering Mathematics I & II, Engineering Physics, Engineering Chemistry, Professional Communication, and Environmental Studies.

Makerspace and Ideation Lab
Hosts the central Product Realization and Makerspace lab equipped with 3D printers, laser cutters, CNC milling machines, and PCB fabrication units, introducing freshman students to design thinking and rapid prototyping.
