SP-TBI Technology Incubator
Sardar Patel Technology Business Incubator (SP-TBI) is an initiative supported by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India. It provides budding student and faculty startups with co-working space, seed funding, intellectual property guidance, legal advisory, and high-profile investor pitching opportunities.

E-Cell (Entrepreneurship Cell)
The student-run E-Cell organizes E-Summit, startup expos, business plan competitions, and founder talk shows, cultivating a venture creation mindset among the student body.
