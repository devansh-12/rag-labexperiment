Overview and Courses
Established in 1995, the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (EXTC) offers B.Tech. in EXTC (intake 120), M.Tech. in EXTC, and Ph.D. The department is NBA Tier-I accredited and emphasizes embedded systems, signal processing, and 5G wireless networks.

Facilities and Specializations
Equipped with Keysight RF Vector Signal Analyzers, Cadence VLSI EDA tools, Texas Instruments DSP and IoT kits, and MATLAB campus-wide licenses. Research tracks include IoT Architectures, VLSI Chip Design, Optical Communication, and Embedded AI.
