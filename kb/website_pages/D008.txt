Overview of CSE-DS and AIML
To meet the burgeoning demand for data-centric and machine intelligence engineering, SPIT launched the B.Tech. in CSE (Data Science) with an intake of 60 students and B.Tech. in CSE (AI & Machine Learning). The curriculum blends statistical foundations, deep neural architectures, MLOps, and big data pipelines.

Infrastructure and Partnerships
GPU computing servers with CUDA support, industry mentorship from leading data science enterprises, and hands-on capstone projects spanning healthcare AI, financial analytics, and autonomous systems.
