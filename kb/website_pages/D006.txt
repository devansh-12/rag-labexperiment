Academic Programmes
The Department of Information Technology offers an undergraduate B.Tech. in Information Technology with an annual intake of 60 students. It prepares engineers in web and enterprise software systems, information security, data engineering, and ubiquitous cloud architectures.

Research and Laboratories
Laboratories include: Enterprise Software Systems Lab, Mobile Computing Lab, Information Security and Cryptography Lab, and Data Analytics Lab. Faculty and students regularly publish papers in IEEE, Springer, and ACM conferences.
