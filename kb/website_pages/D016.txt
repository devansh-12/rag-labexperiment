Professional Student Chapters
Active student chapters include IEEE SPIT Student Branch, Computer Society of India (CSI) SPIT Chapter, ACM Student Chapter, and IETE Student Forum. These bodies organize technical seminars, coding contests, and paper presentation conferences.

Cultural and Social Clubs
Rotaract Club of SPIT spearheads community outreach, blood donation drives, and environmental sustainability campaigns. Mudra is the cultural society encompassing music, drama, fine arts, and literary events.
