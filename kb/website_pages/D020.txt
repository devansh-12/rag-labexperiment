Continuous Assessment Components
Evaluation for each theory course comprises: In-Semester Evaluation (ISE) carrying 20% weightage (quizzes, micro-projects, assignments); Mid-Semester Examination (MSE) carrying 30% weightage (conducted at mid-term for 1.5 hours); and End-Semester Examination (ESE) carrying 50% weightage (comprehensive 3-hour examination covering the entire syllabus).

Grading System and SGPA/CGPA Calculation
SPIT uses a 10-point absolute grading scale: O (Outstanding, 10 grade points, >=85 marks), A+ (Excellent, 9 points, 75-84), A (Very Good, 8 points, 70-74), B+ (Good, 7 points, 60-69), B (Above Average, 6 points, 55-59), C (Average, 5 points, 50-54), P (Pass, 4 points, 40-49), and F (Fail, 0 points, <40 marks). Semester Grade Performance Average (SGPA) and Cumulative Grade Performance Average (CGPA) are computed accordingly.
