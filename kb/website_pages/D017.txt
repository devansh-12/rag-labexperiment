Oculus – National Techno-Cultural Festival
Oculus is the flagship annual national-level techno-cultural festival of SPIT attracting over 8,000 students across the country. It features high-stakes hackathons, robotics challenges, coding marathons, gaming leagues, alongside musical performances, dance battles, and fashion showcases.

Spoorthi – Annual Sports Olympiad
Spoorthi is the inter-collegiate and intra-collegiate sports tournament celebrating athletic prowess in cricket, football, basketball, badminton, table tennis, athletics, and chess.
