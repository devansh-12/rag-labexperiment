Attendance Requirement
A student must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in aggregate across all registered courses in each semester to be eligible to appear for the End-Semester Examination. Condonation up to 10% (i.e. relaxation down to 65%) may be granted by the Principal exclusively on genuine medical grounds accompanied by registered hospital certificates.

Allowed to Keep Terms (ATKT) Criteria
A student is permitted to keep term (progress to the next academic year) only if the total number of failing credit backlogs at the end of the preceding year does not exceed the threshold set by the Academic Council (maximum of 4 failed heads of passing in preceding semesters).
