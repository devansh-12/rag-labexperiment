Seed Funding and Incentives
SPIT awards internal faculty research seed grants up to Rs. 2,00,000 for innovative interdisciplinary exploratory studies. Financial incentives and travel reimbursement are provided for presenting papers at premier CORE A/A* and IEEE/ACM conferences.

Patent and IPR Support
The institute bears 100% of filing and examination fees for patents, copyrights, and industrial designs originating from student-faculty collaborative projects through the SPIT IPR Cell.
