NAAC and NIRF Rankings
Sardar Patel Institute of Technology is accredited with 'A+' Grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). In the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) Engineering Category by Ministry of Education, Government of India, SPIT is consistently ranked among the top engineering institutes in Mumbai and Maharashtra.

NBA Accreditation
All undergraduate programs (Computer Engineering, Information Technology, Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering) are accredited under the prestigious Tier-I Washington Accord format by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA).
